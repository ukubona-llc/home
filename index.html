Ukubona LLC 🌍🛟#

A Platform for Personalized Risk, Navigational Decision Support, and Open Knowledge Architecture

“To see clearly is to act wisely.” — African proverb

Welcome to Ukubona LLC, a public benefit company building an open platform for personalized decision support in clinical and public domains. Our mission is to empower individuals and professionals with tools that translate complex data into clear, interactive, and context-specific insights.

🎯 Why Ukubona?#

Ukubona means “to see” in Zulu—but this is not passive vision. It implies discernment, interpretation, and the capacity to act under uncertainty.

Every domain that generates sufficiently granular data—from medicine to education to policy—faces the same challenge:

How do we transform big data into small, actionable decisions?

Ukubona offers an extensible, modular answer to that question.

🔧 What the Platform Does#

Ukubona allows users to input structured data (e.g., via forms, voice input, or API) and outputs dynamic risk visualizations using:

  • Kaplan-Meier curves

  • Multivariable Cox regression

  • Bayesian overlays (future)

  • Scenario comparisons

The platform is domain-agnostic. It is currently demonstrated using clinical data for living kidney donors, but its engine is portable to:

  • Oncology survival estimates

  • Cardiovascular risk tools

  • Surgical outcome prediction

  • Educational outcomes

  • Climate risk modeling

  • Social policy simulations

Anywhere there is enough data, Ukubona can model consequence.

🧪 Current Demo: Living Kidney Donors#

As a live demo, Ukubona currently features a model built from U.S. national registry data to estimate the perioperative and long-term risks for older living kidney donors.

This prototype emerged from the doctoral research and NIH K08-funded work of Dr. Abimereki Muzaale and has been adapted into a web app. But it is just one use case—a proof-of-concept, not a limit.

🧾 STEM OPT | E-Verify | Public Mission#

Ukubona LLC is:

  • ✅ A registered E-Verify employer

  • ✅ The sponsoring site for a STEM OPT Training Plan (Form I-983)

  • ✅ Building in public, with open-source transparency

  • ✅ Centered on applied data science, epidemiology, and ethical design

This Jupyter Book serves as an evolving scientific portfolio, training log, and platform narrative. Analyst: Vincent Jin. Supervisor: Dr. Abimereki Muzaale.
